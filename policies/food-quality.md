# Food quality complaints
This covers food that is stale, undercooked, oily, tasteless or otherwise poor. Quality is subjective and cannot be verified from order data.

Ask the customer for a photo of the food. With a photo: refund up to the price of the affected item, as decided by a human agent. Without a photo: do not propose a refund; ask for the photo.

For cold food, first read the cold-food policy.
