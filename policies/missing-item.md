# Missing item
A missing item is verified when the order data shows that item with packed set to false. Refund the price of each missing item times its quantity. Do not refund the delivery fee or taxes.

If every item in the order shows packed as true, the claim cannot be verified from data. Do not propose a refund. Ask a human agent to review, and ask the customer for a photo of the delivered bag.

If the customer names an item that is not in the order at all, treat the claim as unverifiable.
