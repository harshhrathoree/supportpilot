# Late delivery compensation
Lateness is the actual delivery time minus the promised time. Use the minutes_late value from the order data. Do not trust the customer's own estimate of how late it was.

| Minutes late | Compensation |
|---|---|
| 15 or fewer | None. This counts as on time. Apologise. |
| 16 to 30 | Credit of 10% of the order value, up to Rs 50. |
| 31 to 60 | Credit of 20% of the order value, up to Rs 100. |
| More than 60 | Refund of 50% of the order value, up to Rs 300. |

If the order data shows the order was not late, no compensation applies, even if the customer says it was.
