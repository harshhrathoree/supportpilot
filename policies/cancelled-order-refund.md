# Cancelled orders and refund status
If the order status is cancelled: the full amount is refunded when the order was cancelled before the restaurant accepted it, or when the restaurant or the company cancelled it. If the customer cancelled after preparation started, 50% of the order value is refunded.

Cash on delivery orders have nothing to refund. When a customer asks where their refund is, check the order status and payment method first, then follow the refund-timelines policy.
