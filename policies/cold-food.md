# Cold food complaints
Cold food is usually explained by a slow delivery. Check delivery_minutes in the order data.

If delivery took more than 60 minutes from the time the order was placed, treat the complaint as a delay. Apply the late-delivery policy, not the food-quality policy.

If delivery took 60 minutes or less, the food should not have gone cold from the delay alone. Follow the food-quality policy and ask for a photo.
